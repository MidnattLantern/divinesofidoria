## AI disclosure
https://no-ai-icon.com/statement/?url=midnattlantern.github.io/divinesofidoria
I remain committed to distancing myself from AI and LLMs by minimizing my use of them. Claude & Grammarly AI have been involved strictly for looking up technical information, when traditional video tutorials and reading in forums such as Reddit, Stack Overflow or YouTube fall short. This includes TypeScript flags or syntax, Vite documentation, grammar check, technical Blender jargons, or oddly specific gotchas. However, no code, nor any vector and raster assets were AI generated.
